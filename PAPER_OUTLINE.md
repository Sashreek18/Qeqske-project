# Paper Outline: Calibrated Machine-Learning Side-Channel Auditing of Post-Quantum Key Exchange (QEQSKE vs. ML-KEM-512)

## Working Title
**Mind the Profiler: Calibrated Machine-Learning Side-Channel Auditing of Post-Quantum Key Exchange from Toy Prototypes to ML-KEM-512**

## Abstract (Draft Summary)
Machine-learning (ML) classifiers are increasingly used to audit post-quantum cryptographic implementations for timing, memory, and channel side-channels, yet naive pipelines suffer from uncalibrated noise floors, public-parameter confounds, and profiler-induced observer artifacts. Using HCL Software's Lattice/LWE-based QEQSKE key exchange protocol alongside FIPS 203 ML-KEM-512 (`kyber-py`) across 1,106,944 ANU QRNG draws (`qrng_combined.txt`) and two physical architectures (`Linux x86_64 Intel Core i9-12900K` and `macOS arm64 Apple M2`), we present a calibrated five-model ML auditing methodology with leak-free in-fold confound residualization (`QResidualizer`), shuffled-label controls, 200-permutation tests, and graded delay injection. Our evaluation establishes four empirical findings:
1. Unmodified pure-Python `decrypt_it()` at toy parameters (`n=4, k=2`) leaks `secret_weight` consistently across same-seed cross-host tests (`seed=42`, `53.70%` baseline) — **65.78%** on Linux `i9-12900K` (`results/decrypt_leak_5000_linux_i9_recollect.json`, runtime-captured environment, `R² = +0.2127`, `ρ = +0.4237`, `p < 1/201`; earlier legacy-provenance run `results/decrypt_leak_5000.json` [*environment inferred, not captured at run time*]: `65.25%`, `R² = +0.2260`, `ρ = +0.4166`) and **63.65%** on Apple M2 (`results/decrypt_leak_5000_mac_arm64.json`, `R² = +0.1768`, `p < 1/201`) — reproduces on fresh key sampling (`seed=1337`, `51.54%` baseline: **65.92%** `MLP`, `R² = +0.2070`, `ρ = +0.4099`, `results/decrypt_leak_5000_linux_i9_seed1337.json`), is explained by CPython's cached `x * 0` fast path (`34.25 → 44.25 µs` across weights `0..8`, `results/decrypt_hypothesis_ct.json`), and attenuates under live localhost TCP framing noise to **55.67%** (`ρ = +0.0700`, `results/stress_test_traces_5000_summary.md`).
2. Python's `tracemalloc` acts as a severe **observer effect**, inflating decryption latency `~11.6–11.9×` and artificially boosting classifier accuracy from `63.65–65.92%` to **`84.01–86.33%`** (`84.01%` in `results/decrypt_leak_5000_linux_i9_recollect.json`, `86.33%` in `results/decrypt_leak_5000_linux_i9_seed1337.json`, `84.37%` in `results/decrypt_leak_5000.json`, `85.21%` in `results/decrypt_leak_5000_mac_arm64.json`).
3. Replacing pure-Python arithmetic with branch-free vectorized NumPy (`decrypt_it_constant_time`) removes the leak to baseline (`53.56%`, `p = 0.7065`, all `R² < 0` in `results/decrypt_ct_leak_5000.json`; `53.92%` over TCP, `p = 0.0995` in `results/stress_test_traces_5000.json`), while isolating a `~0.256 µs` signed floor-modulo (`% q64`) micro-trend (`results/decrypt_hypothesis_ct.json`).
4. Scaling to real `n=256` NTT-domain ML-KEM-512 (`standard` and per-key `q` QRNG `QEQSKE` variant) extinguishes the coefficient-sparsity leak on both fixed seed-42 keys (`52.12%` / `51.09%`, `p >= 0.1592`, unchanged under rolling-median detrending `52.01%` / `51.03%`, `results/mlkem512_decrypt_leak_5000.json`, `results/mlkem512_qeqske_decrypt_leak_5000.json`, `results/mlkem512_detrend_comparison.json`) and fresh seed-1337 keys (`51.89%` vs. `51.68%` `p = 0.1244` and `52.52%` vs. `51.94%` `p = 0.0697`, all `R² < 0`, `results/mlkem512_linux_i9_seed1337_decrypt_leak_5000.json`, `results/mlkem512_qeqske_linux_i9_seed1337_decrypt_leak_5000.json`). Calibrated `n=256` positive controls bound any undetected leak below **`5.0 µs`** on Linux `i9-12900K` (`results/mlkem512_positive_control_5000_linux_i9.json`, ran under the `powersave` governor / `intel_pstate` scaling driver, `41.3 µs` IQR) and **`2.0 µs`** on Apple M2 (`results/mlkem512_positive_control_5000.json`, `16.3 µs` IQR).

## 1. Introduction & Threat Model
- **Research Question:** Where do exploitable timing, execution, and channel leaks emerge across an end-to-end post-quantum key exchange (Sender `encrypt_it`, Public Channel `(A, t, u, v_queue, q)`, Receiver `decrypt_it`), and how do ML auditing pipelines avoid both false positives (observer artifacts, public-modulus confounds) and false negatives (uncalibrated noise floors)?
- **Threat Model Tiers:**
  1. *QRNG / Channel Eavesdropper (`Tier 1c`, `Attack A/B`):* Passive observer inspecting ANU QRNG outputs and serialized ciphertexts `(u, v_queue, q)`.
  2. *Local Co-Resident Software Timing Observer (`Tier 1a/1b`, `Attack C`):* Unprivileged co-resident attacker measuring `perf_counter_ns` / `ru_utime` across sender encapsulation and receiver decapsulation.
  3. *Network Timing Observer (`Tier 1d/1e`):* Active sender / on-path observer measuring round-trip ACK latency over persistent TCP (`127.0.0.1`, `TCP_NODELAY=1`).
  4. *Excluded Scope:* Invasive fault injection (`Attack D`, noted only as a missing FO-check appendix observation in `attack_d_cca_mismatch.py`) and hardware EM/power probing.

## 2. Related Work
- ML-based side-channel analysis on lattice KEMs (timing & cache attacks on Kyber / NewHope; KyberSlash constant-time division vulnerabilities).
- Variable-time big-integer and sparse-operand timing leaks in interpreted runtimes (CPython `PyLongObject` small-int cache and allocation hooks).
- Methodological pitfalls in ML side-channel evaluation: out-of-fold vs. in-fold confound residualization, thermal/frequency drift in high-precision timers, host-specific noise floors, and permutation resolution floors (`1/(N+1)`).

## 3. Evaluation Methodology
- **Datasets & Randomness Governance:**
  - `1,106,944` ANU QRNG `uint16` draws (`qrng_combined.txt`); `mlkem512_qeqske` consumes `245,000` draws (`wraps = 0`, zero reuse in `results/mlkem512_detrend_comparison.json`); toy `n=4` consumes `4,905,000` draws (`wraps = 4`, yielding `5,000/5,000` unique `(q, s)` pairs at `seed=42` in `results/decrypt_leak_5000_linux_i9_recollect.json` and `4,999/5,000` at `seed=1337` in `results/decrypt_leak_5000_linux_i9_seed1337.json`).
  - **Machine-only vs. fresh-key experimental design:** Cross-host comparisons using identical `seed=42` (`results/decrypt_leak_5000_mac_arm64.json`, `results/decrypt_leak_5000_linux_i9_recollect.json`, `results/decrypt_leak_5000.json`) test **only the host/runtime** on fixed keys; `--seed 1337` runs (`qrng_start_offset = 795,881`, `SHAKE-256 seed=1337`) test **fresh key sampling** on independent secret/modulus draws (`results/decrypt_leak_5000_linux_i9_seed1337.json`, `results/mlkem512_linux_i9_seed1337_decrypt_leak_5000.json`, `results/mlkem512_qeqske_linux_i9_seed1337_decrypt_leak_5000.json`).
- **Trace Collection Protocol:** Uninstrumented primary pass (`perf_counter` / `perf_counter_ns` + `ru_utime`) isolated from `tracemalloc` observer pass; randomized per-pass trial order, separate Mersenne warm-up pool (`seed=42000 / 43337`, defined in `attack_c_decrypt_5000.py:206` and `attack_c_mlkem_decrypt_5000.py:462`), interleaved GC + warm-up blocks, and CPU core 0 pinning (`os.sched_setaffinity`) on `Linux`.
- **Drift Mitigation at `n=256`:** Compare Raw vs. linear Order-OLS vs. leave-one-out centered Rolling-Median (`W=51`) residuals (`attack_c_mlkem_decrypt_5000.py --detrend-existing`).
- **Statistical & ML Pipeline (`5` Model Families):** Logistic/Linear Regression, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, SVM/SVR (RBF), MLP evaluated over `RepeatedStratifiedKFold(n_splits=5, n_repeats=10)` (classification `secret_weight > median`) and `RepeatedKFold(5x10)` (continuous regression `R²`), validated against majority-class baseline, shuffled-label control, leak-free in-fold `QResidualizer`, `2,000`-resample paired bootstrap 95% CIs, and `200`-permutation tests (`< 1/201 (0.0050)` resolution floor).
- **Per-Host Positive Control Calibration:** Inject secret-dependent busy-wait delays (`0.0–5.0 µs` on `decrypt_it_constant_time`; `0, 1, 2, 5, 10, 20, 50 µs` on `ML-KEM-512` `decaps`) on each architecture separately rather than transferring an Apple M2 floor onto Linux measurements.

## 4. Empirical Results
1. **Toy QEQSKE (`n=4, k=2`) Uninstrumented Timing Leak Across Hosts & Seeds:**
   - **Linux `i9-12900K` runtime-captured recollection (`seed=42`, same keys, `results/decrypt_leak_5000_linux_i9_recollect.json`):** `73.37 ± 5.47 µs`, **`65.78% ± 1.51%`** (`SVM (RBF)`, `AUC 0.7096`, `p < 1/201`), `R² = +0.2127 ± 0.0272` (`MLP`, `p < 1/201`), `ρ = +0.4237` timing / `+0.4060` CPU.
   - **Linux `i9-12900K` earlier run (`seed=42`, *environment inferred, not captured at run time*, `results/decrypt_leak_5000.json`):** `75.21 ± 6.37 µs`, **`65.25% ± 1.49%`** (`MLP`, `AUC 0.7076`), `R² = +0.2260 ± 0.0256` (`SVR (RBF)`), `ρ = +0.4166` timing / `+0.4023` CPU (`p < 1/201`).
   - **macOS `Apple M2` (`seed=42`, same keys, `results/decrypt_leak_5000_mac_arm64.json`):** `76.79 ± 6.34 µs`, **`63.65% ± 1.30%`** (`Grad Boost`, `AUC 0.6798`), `R² = +0.1768 ± 0.0327` (`MLP`), `ρ = +0.3418` timing / `+0.3706` CPU (`p < 1/201`).
   - **Linux `i9-12900K` fresh key sampling (`seed=1337`, `qrng_start_offset=795,881`, baseline `51.54%`, `results/decrypt_leak_5000_linux_i9_seed1337.json`):** `72.78 ± 5.90 µs`, **`65.92% ± 1.44%`** (`MLP`, `+14.38` pts over baseline, `AUC 0.7063`, `p < 1/201`), `R² = +0.2070 ± 0.0300` (`MLP`, `p < 1/201`), `ρ = +0.4099` timing / `+0.3914` CPU.
2. **End-to-End Sender ↔ Receiver & Localhost TCP RTT (`5,000` clean trials, `Apple M2`, `results/stress_test_traces_5000_summary.md`):**
   - Sender `53.68%` (`p = 0.3682`); Receiver Local `65.29%` (`SVM`, `p < 1/201`); Public Channel `55.59%` (`p < 1/201`) — removing the `119/5,000` (`2.38%`) all-zero-column keys eliminates most of the channel signal, but `ct_entropy_approx` still correlates with secret weight (`r = +0.1125, p = 3e-15`) in `results/tier1c_zero_column_analysis.json`; TCP Orig attenuated to `55.67%` (`ρ = +0.0700`, `p < 1/201`) vs. TCP Constant-Time `53.92%` (`ρ = -0.0033`, `p = 0.0995`).
3. **The `tracemalloc` Observer Effect (`~11.6–11.9×` Runtime & `+18.2–21.6 pt` Accuracy Inflation):**
   - Clean → `tracemalloc`: `75.21 → 882.02 µs` / `65.25% → 84.37%` (`results/decrypt_leak_5000.json`, *environment inferred, not captured at run time*); `73.37 → 852.27 µs` / `65.78% → 84.01%` (`ρ = +0.8290`, `R² = +0.6960`, `results/decrypt_leak_5000_linux_i9_recollect.json`); `72.78 → 864.43 µs` / `65.92% → 86.33%` (`ρ = +0.8615`, `R² = +0.7445`, `results/decrypt_leak_5000_linux_i9_seed1337.json`); `76.79 → 892.15 µs` / `63.65% → 85.21%` (`results/decrypt_leak_5000_mac_arm64.json`), while `ram_peak_bytes` / `num_allocations` add negligible direct predictive power.
4. **Root-Cause Isolation & Constant-Time Mitigation:**
   - `cProfile` (`mat_mult_mod` = `42.0%` runtime, `82%` of paired `w=8 − w=0` gap) + pinned microbenchmark (`34.25 → 44.25 µs`, `ρ = +0.7306`; `x*0 = 11.01 ns` vs. `x*±1 = 16.15–16.54 ns`, `results/decrypt_hypothesis_ct.json`).
   - `decrypt_it_constant_time` eliminates the leak (`53.56%`, `ρ = -0.0258`, `p = 0.7065`, all `R² < 0`, `results/decrypt_ct_leak_5000.json`); component isolation uncovers signed NumPy floor-modulo branch (`+0.256 µs`, `ρ = +0.3393`) removed by non-negative offset `+4*q64` (`ρ = -0.0186`, `results/decrypt_hypothesis_ct.json`).
   - Toy sensitivity floor: `0.5 µs` (`61.62%` Full 8D / `60.12%` Decrypt 4D, `p < 1/201`, `results/attack_c_leaky_vs_clean.json`).
5. **Scaling to Real ML-KEM-512 (`n=256`, `kyber-py`) + QEQSKE Variant Across Seeds & Positive Control Floors:**
   - **Seed `42` (`results/mlkem512_decrypt_leak_5000.json`, `results/mlkem512_qeqske_decrypt_leak_5000.json`):** NTT destroys operand zero-sparsity (`160/512` zeros in `s` → `0.04–0.15/512` in `s_hat`). Both Standard (`52.12%`, `p = 0.1592`) and QEQSKE-Variant (`51.09%`, `p = 0.2438`, despite `q`-timing `r = +0.1493` cleanly controlled by `QResidualizer`) sit at baseline (`all R² < 0`). Rolling-median detrending (`W=51`, `results/mlkem512_detrend_comparison.json`) removes execution-order drift (`ρ -0.4668 → -0.0108`) and leaves both strictly at baseline (`52.01%` `p = 0.3234`, `51.03%` `p = 0.3731`).
   - **Seed `1337` fresh keys (`results/mlkem512_linux_i9_seed1337_decrypt_leak_5000.json`, `results/mlkem512_qeqske_linux_i9_seed1337_decrypt_leak_5000.json`):** Both remain strictly at baseline — Standard **`51.89%`** vs. `51.68%` (`p = 0.1244`, `ρ_wt = -0.0113`, all `R² < 0`, `s[0][0]>0` `66.34%` vs `66.34%`) and QEQSKE-Variant **`52.52%`** vs. `51.94%` (`p = 0.0697`, `ρ_wt = -0.0120`, all `R² < 0`, `s[0][0]>0` `65.38%` vs `65.38%`).
   - **Per-Host `n=256` Positive Controls (`0–50 µs`):** On **Linux `i9-12900K`** (`results/mlkem512_positive_control_5000_linux_i9.json`, `powersave` governor, `2084.8 µs` median, `41.3 µs` raw IQR / `36.6 µs` detrended IQR), delays `<= 2.0 µs` are undetectable (`51.98%`, `p = 0.5522`) and the detection floor is **`5.0 µs`** (`54.36%` Raw MLP / `54.16%` Detrended MLP, `ρ_bin = +0.0844` / `+0.0830`, `p < 1/201`). Thus the **Linux `n=256` null is bounded below `< 5.0 µs` (`< 0.25%` of `decaps`)**. Independently, on **Apple M2** (`results/mlkem512_positive_control_5000.json`, `2382.3 µs` median, `16.3 µs` IQR), the tighter jitter lowers that platform's floor to **`2.0 µs`** (`54.10%` Raw MLP / `55.40%` Detrended SVM, `p < 1/201`).

## 5. Limitations
1. **Software Timing Proxies Only:** Evaluates `perf_counter_ns`, `ru_utime`, `tracemalloc`, and loopback TCP RTT — not hardware PMU counters (`perf`), oscilloscope power traces, or EM probes; absence of timing leakage does not imply DPA/CPA power resistance without masking.
2. **Pure-Python Target Implementations (`qeqske_core.py` & `kyber-py`):** Educational Python implementations execute in microseconds/milliseconds with interpreter allocation jitter rather than cycle-accurate compiled C/AVX2 assembly.
3. **Toy Parameters (`n=4, k=2`) Are Algebraically Insecure:** Secret matrices at `n=4` are directly brute-forceable from public `(A, t)` in `<1 ms` (`break_poc.py`, `attack_e_algebraic_lwe.py`, `results/break_poc_and_algebraic_lwe.json`), and removing the `119/5,000` (`2.38%`) all-zero-column keys eliminates most of the channel signal, but `ct_entropy_approx` still correlates with secret weight (`r = +0.1125, p = 3e-15`) in `results/tier1c_zero_column_analysis.json` (`Tier 1c` in `results/stress_test_traces_5000.json`); toy results probe timing side-channel mechanics, not LWE hardness.
4. **Fresh-Key Global Secret Weight vs. Fixed-Key Full Recovery:** Primary ML targets binary/continuous global `secret_weight` (plus `s[0][0]` sign at `n=256`) from 1 trace per fresh key rather than multi-query template/CPA recovery of every secret coefficient against a single static key.
5. **Localhost Loopback TCP vs. Real Network Jitter:** Localhost `127.0.0.1` TCP (`IQR 34.5 µs` in `results/stress_test_traces_5000.json`) already attenuates the local leak (`65.29% → 55.67%`); wide-area internet jitter (milliseconds) would overwhelm sub-10 µs software timing differences.
6. **QRNG Stream Reuse on Toy Runs (`4` Wraps):** While `mlkem512_qeqske` uses zero QRNG reuse (`245,000 / 1,106,944`, `wraps = 0` in `results/mlkem512_detrend_comparison.json`), the 5,000-trial toy `n=4` runs cycle `4` times over the `1,106,944`-number stream (`4,905,000` draws from `1,106,944` numbers, `wraps = 4`), so **key randomness is reused** (still producing `5,000/5,000` unique `(q, s)` pairs at `seed=42` and `4,999/5,000` at `seed=1337`).
7. **Cross-Architecture Coverage & Linux Governor Noise:** Core toy replication spans Linux `i9-12900K` and Apple M2 across both fixed (`seed=42`) and fresh (`seed=1337`) keys. The `n=256` positive-control floor differs materially between hosts (`5.0 µs` on Linux `i9-12900K`, which ran under the `powersave` governor, `results/mlkem512_positive_control_5000_linux_i9.json`, vs. `2.0 µs` on Apple M2, `results/mlkem512_positive_control_5000.json`), and **the governor is not recorded in the existing Linux result files** (`env_metadata.py` records it only for new runs).
8. **Toy-Scale Leak Magnitude vs. Linux `n=256` Detection Floor:** In `results/decrypt_traces_5000_linux_i9_recollect.json`, the binary weight split (`secret_weight > 5` median `73.46 µs` vs. `<= 5` median `71.76 µs`) corresponds to an absolute median timing shift of **`1.70 µs`** (`1.68 µs` mean shift); thus a leak of the toy's absolute size would sit below the **`5.0 µs`** Linux detection floor at `n=256` (`results/mlkem512_positive_control_5000_linux_i9.json`).

## 6. Changes after Linux rerun
1. **Linux `n=256` Detection Floor Revised from `2.0 µs` to `5.0 µs`:** Running `--positive-control` natively on Linux `i9-12900K` (`results/mlkem512_positive_control_5000_linux_i9.json`, `powersave` governor) showed `2.0 µs` is *not* detectable on Linux (`51.98%` Raw, `p = 0.5522`; `41.3 µs` raw IQR vs. `16.3 µs` on Apple M2), moving the Linux null bound from the earlier reused Apple M2 `2.0 µs` figure to **`5.0 µs`** (`54.36%` Raw MLP / `54.16%` Detrended MLP, `p < 1/201`).
2. **Legacy vs. Runtime-Captured Linux Toy Traces (`results/decrypt_leak_5000.json` vs. `results/decrypt_leak_5000_linux_i9_recollect.json`):** The earlier `results/decrypt_leak_5000.json` (`65.25%` MLP, `R² = +0.2260` SVR, `ρ = +0.4166`) is now explicitly labelled **`"environment inferred, not captured at run time"`**; fresh runtime-captured recollection on the identical machine and seed (`results/decrypt_leak_5000_linux_i9_recollect.json`) confirms the leak at **`65.78%`** (`SVM (RBF)`), `R² = +0.2127` (`MLP`), `ρ = +0.4237` (`p < 1/201`).
3. **Seed-`42` Machine-Only Check vs. Seed-`1337` Fresh Key Sampling:** Same-seed (`seed=42`) runs across Apple M2 and Linux `i9-12900K` are now explicitly framed as machine-only comparisons on identical keys, while fresh-key invariance is established separately by `--seed 1337` (`results/decrypt_leak_5000_linux_i9_seed1337.json` reproducing toy leakage at `65.92%` vs. `51.54%` baseline, `R² = +0.2070`, `ρ = +0.4099`; `results/mlkem512_linux_i9_seed1337_decrypt_leak_5000.json` and `results/mlkem512_qeqske_linux_i9_seed1337_decrypt_leak_5000.json` confirming `n=256` stays at baseline).
4. **Updated `--detrend-existing` Summary Alignment:** `reproduce_all.py --mode fast` updated `results/mlkem512_detrend_comparison.json` (`Standard` Raw best `52.12%` `SVM` vs `52.02%` baseline, Rolling-Median `p = 0.3234`).
